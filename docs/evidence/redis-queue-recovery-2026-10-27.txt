Environment: Lightsail development VPS; APP_ENV=test; PostgreSQL database ctdmp_test; a unique test-only BullMQ prefix. WhatsApp remained MOCK/DRY_RUN; no external provider, real recipient, or charge was involved.

## Change

- Extracted pending-notification queue reconstruction to packages/shared/dispatch-queue.ts.
- The worker scans a bounded batch of PostgreSQL notifications still in PLANNED and submits BullMQ jobs using stable notification-{id} job IDs.
- A queue retry after partial enqueue is deduplicated by BullMQ job IDs. dispatchOne still locks the notification and rechecks current consent, freshness, state, template, pause, and budget before preparation.
- Prepared/suppressed/expired notifications are excluded from queue recovery by the PostgreSQL state predicate.

## Limits

This is a deterministic test of losing BullMQ queue state using Queue.obliterate on an isolated prefix. It does not restart or flush the shared Redis server, prove Redis host failover, or establish production delivery guarantees. Existing message-provider behavior remains mock/dry-run only.
