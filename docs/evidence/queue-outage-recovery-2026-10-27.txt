# Queue outage recovery check — 27 October 2026

Environment: isolated ctdmp_test PostgreSQL database and a deterministic in-memory BullMQ adapter. No production queue, provider, or member data was used.

## OPS-002 scenario and result

- Created two fictional, opted-in recipients with durable PLANNED notifications.
- Injected a broker failure after the first stable notification job ID was accepted but before the second enqueue completed.
- Confirmed the enqueue cycle reported BROKER_UNAVAILABLE and both notification rows remained PLANNED in PostgreSQL.
- Restored the simulated broker and reran the bounded PostgreSQL scan. Both stable job IDs were present, including the first accepted job without duplication.
- Repeated recovery and confirmed the mock queue still held exactly two logical job IDs.
- VPS `pnpm check`: TypeScript passed; 37/37 PostgreSQL/API tests passed, including OPS-001 and OPS-002.

## Limits

This deterministic adapter test checks application behavior around a partial queue outage. It does not stop or fail over the Redis server, exercise real BullMQ reconnection, or prove production availability. A complete isolated Redis host outage/failover drill remains open. PostgreSQL remains the durable source of notification work.
