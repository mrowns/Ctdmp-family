Implemented:
- Migration 013 adds organization-scoped normalized usernames, pending registrations and the ACCOUNT_CREATION WebAuthn challenge purpose.
- Signup creates an inactive member and pending record. The server activates it only after a verified resident passkey ceremony with user verification.
- Pending signup expires after 30 minutes and bounded worker cleanup deletes the inactive member and dependent signup records.
- Signup has a reserved-name policy, no branch or role grant, and inserts live_start=false; notification consent is not part of account creation.
- The browser flow creates a fictional account, registers a first passkey, adds a second credential using an independent virtual authenticator, signs out, and completes discoverable sign-in.
- Public app environments remain blocked; no real identities, channels, messages, or providers were used.
